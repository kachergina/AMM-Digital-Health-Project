Healthcare innovation concept

Safe admission for patients who need it most

AMM is an isolated, doctor-supervised gateway at the hospital entrance — designed for rapid assessment, sanitary treatment, and stabilization of patients from socially vulnerable groups: homeless individuals, intoxicated patients, those without ID, and people in poor hygiene.

Why this matters

Tens of thousands of homeless people in Russia seek care for injuries, frostbite, poisoning, and untreated chronic disease — yet hospitals lack safe admission infrastructure. Three barriers collide at once:

Epidemiological

Tuberculosis, hepatitis, HIV, skin and parasitic diseases risk immunocompromised patients in shared wards.

Behavioral

Intoxication-driven aggression puts healthcare workers — often unprotected — at risk of injury and burnout.

Social-hygienic

No documents, no medical history, severe contamination blocking examination and IV access.

Built from proven technology

AMM does not invent new science — it integrates da Vinci-style manipulators, hospital service robots, UV disinfection, smart diagnostic concepts, and caregiving robotics into one admission gateway.

⚙

Robotic manipulator

How the module works

From ambulance arrival to hospital transfer — every step is protocol-driven and doctor-approved.

1

Admission & isolation

Patient enters the sealed module. External door locks. AMM switches to autonomous mode under remote supervision.

2

Non-contact assessment

Scanner collects vitals, thermal data, and 3D injury mapping. System classifies: cooperative, unconscious, or aggressive.

3

Contact examination

Robotic arm inserts IV, draws blood, starts saline, attaches pulse oximeter and blood pressure cuff.

4

Doctor decision

Full data panel: 3D model, thermal scan, vitals, lab results. Doctor communicates via audio and approves every action.

5

Stabilization

Doctor-approved treatment: glucose for hypoglycemia, soft restraints if needed, robotic shower and warm-air drying for sanitary care.

6

Transfer & sterilization

Patient exits via sealed corridor. Module locks, disinfects with antiseptic and UV. Ready for next admission in ~15 minutes.

Research hypothesis

Doctor-supervised AMM units as isolated admission gateways should measurably improve safety, speed, and capacity.

Faster assessment — vitals and primary data in less time than standard admission
Safer staff — fewer injuries from aggressive or contaminated patients
Lower infection risk — isolation plus standardized disinfection protocols
Higher throughput — redistributed procedures, less staff time per complex patient

Protocol Delta

For uncooperative or aggressive patients — a humane de-escalation path that minimizes physical contact between staff and patient while keeping the doctor in full control.

Read full protocol
1. Verbal intervention — calm doctor communication via speakers
2. Soft mechanical restraint — padded cushions, no pain
3. Inhalation sedation — controlled gas, monitored SpO2
4. Return to standard protocol — safe IV and diagnostics

The doctor always decides

AMM is a tool, not a replacement. Every procedure requires explicit physician approval.
